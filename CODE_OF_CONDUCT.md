# Community code of conduct

Participants must communicate respectfully, critique evidence rather than people, protect personal information, and avoid harassment or discriminatory conduct. Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions that violate these expectations.

Policy disagreement is expected. The project records competing evidence and correction requests without treating good-faith disagreement as misconduct.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer using the public institutional contact listed in `MAINTAINERS.md`.
