Commit history documents ongoing ownership of the interface and pipeline. The project is seeking an independent reproducer and additional security/release reviewers; those roles will not be described as filled until written commitments and public evidence exist.

## Decision process

- Routine changes: maintainer review and automated checks.
- Data corrections: source evidence, recorded disposition, and versioned correction history.
- Security-relevant changes: threat-model update and regression test.
- Schema-breaking changes: documented migration and version increment.
- Claim retirement: the old identifier remains discoverable with a retirement reason and replacement link when available.

## Sustainability target

For twelve months after the first public release, the project intends to publish quarterly maintenance notes, keep the issue tracker open, and document any hosting or model cost that becomes necessary. Funding proposals must identify which maintenance work is covered and which remains unfunded.
