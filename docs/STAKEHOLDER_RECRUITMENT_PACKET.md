# Stakeholder Recruitment and Evidence Packet

## Target roles

Recruit at least five people across at least three roles: classroom educator, district or state leader, family/community advocate, researcher/journalist/civic technologist. Do not describe these participants as representative of all stakeholders.

## Eligibility screener

- Current role and setting
- Experience locating or interpreting school AI guidance
- Accessibility or assistive-technology needs for the session
- Prior involvement with this project
- Conflict or supervisory relationship with the maintainer
- Consent to a de-identified task report; separate optional consent for public attribution

## Neutral invitation

We are evaluating whether a public AI-education policy prototype helps people find the official source behind a displayed claim and recognize uncertainty. This is a usability and evidence-tracing task, not a product endorsement. The system may be incomplete or wrong. We will report failures and corrections in de-identified form. Participation, compensation, recording, and research-status language must be approved through the applicable institutional process before recruitment.

## Minimum evidence to retain

- role category and setting, de-identified;
- task completion and time;
- source located correctly: yes/no;
- uncertainty/review status interpreted correctly: yes/no;
- accessibility barriers encountered;
- correction or missing-context report;
- interviewer deviations; and
- resulting issue, change, or documented no-change rationale.

Do not collect student records, personnel evaluations, or unnecessary sensitive information.

## Fixed evidence-tracing script

Give every participant the same instruction: “Starting from the landing page, find one Oregon claim, open the official source, identify where the claim is supported, and explain what the displayed independent-review status means.” Do not coach after the task begins; record any intervention as assistance.

- `success`: source and anchor are located and review status is interpreted correctly without assistance;
- `partial`: the participant completes only one of those two evidence goals or needs one neutral clarification;
- `failure`: the source/anchor cannot be located, review status is materially misread, or the task is abandoned.

The observation form must record start/end time, clicks or navigation path, assistance, quoted interpretation of review status, source/anchor result, barrier severity, correction request, and de-identification action. Analyze counts by role category only; do not infer representativeness from five participants.
