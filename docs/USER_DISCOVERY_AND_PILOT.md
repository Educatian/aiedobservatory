# User discovery and pilot evidence plan

Public release does not by itself demonstrate public value. Before making adoption or empowerment claims, collect evidence from at least five discovery interviews and one bounded pilot task.

## Roles

- District or state policy staff.
- Classroom educator or educator association representative.
- Public-interest, family-facing, journalist, or civic-data role.

## Interview prompts

Ask about the most recent policy-search task, current source path, time spent, failed searches, stale/conflicting records, the consequence of a wrong summary, and the channel available for correction or appeal. Do not ask only whether the tool seems useful.

## Comparative pilot measures

- Time to locate the controlling source.
- Proportion of answers with a verified primary-source link.
- Stale or superseded policy detection.
- Incorrect extraction detection and correction success.
- Correct interpretation of confidence, review, and integrity states.
- Keyboard, screen-reader, mobile, low-bandwidth, and plain-language task completion.

## Evidence gate

No person or organization will be named as a user, partner, underserved beneficiary, or independent evaluator without permission and a defined task. Interview and pilot results remain pending until collected.
